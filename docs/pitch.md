## Questions to expect

**Why isn't the operating system's "reduce motion" setting enough?**
It only works on sites that chose to support it, and it does not stop GIFs, video or scroll hijacking. Sukoon turns on
that support wherever a site has it (module 2) and handles everything else.

**How is this different from Motion Guard?**
Sukoon is completely free and open, and adds a Flash Guard, independent evidence and an interface built to the same
standard it asks of websites.

**Does it protect against seizures?**
No, and it does not claim to. It reduces exposure to common triggers, and we measure that with a tool built on WCAG.

**Does it break sites?**
Sometimes. That is why we measure the breakage rate and publish it, and why any site can be excluded with one click.

**Where does the data go?**
Nowhere. All analysis happens on the device.
